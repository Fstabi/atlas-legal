Atlas

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Atlas is a geography game for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, who else receives it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect little: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no location.

Who is responsible

Atlas is made by Francesco Stabilini and Manuel Pulido, who decide together how your data is used (joint controllers). For any question or request about your data, write to atlas.geoquiz@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account. If you play as a guest, the app creates an account with a random email address and password, kept on your phone. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address, name and picture of your Google account. To keep your progress and let you sign in again.
Game progress. Stars for each challenge, coins, hearts, unlocked levels, the day you last played the daily challenge, and your multiplayer matches and their results. To run the game and save your progress.
Technical data. Your device's IP address and basic request information, which servers record when the app talks to them. To deliver the service and protect it from abuse.
Settings. Language, theme, sound and map preferences. Kept only on your phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any other data, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase hosts our database and accounts, on servers in the European Union.
  • Google: Google Maps draws the maps in map challenges; Google Sign-In, if you use it; Google Fonts provides the app's typeface; YouTube, if you open a country's music in the Info screen. These services receive your IP address and device information under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Wikipedia, Wikidata, the World Bank and REST Countries provide the facts shown in the Info screen. They receive your IP address when you open it.
  • Other players in a multiplayer match see the results of that match.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union. When they do, they rely on safeguards recognised by EU law, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Why we may use it (legal bases)

We use account and game data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract), and technical data to keep the service secure and working (our legitimate interest).

How long we keep it

  • Guest accounts without progress are deleted after 30 days without use.
  • Guest accounts with progress are deleted after 12 months without use.
  • Google accounts are kept until you delete them.
  • Deleting an account removes its progress, coins, hearts and matches.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Delete account. Your account and everything tied to it are deleted at once and cannot be recovered. Your Google account itself is not affected. If you no longer have the app, write to us and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and object to or restrict its use. Write to atlas.geoquiz@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Children

Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Each player can read and change only their own data.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example when adding new features, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
