Atlas

Delete your account

In the app

Open Settings, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Write to atlas.geoquiz@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you play with, asking to delete your Atlas account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. A guest account cannot be identified without the phone that created it; it is deleted automatically after it stops being used (see below).

What is deleted

Your account, stars, coins, hearts, progress and multiplayer matches. Nothing is kept afterwards. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Unused guest accounts

Guest accounts without progress are deleted after 30 days without use, those with progress after 12 months. More in the Privacy Policy.
